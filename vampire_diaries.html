About the Show

The Vampire Diaries is an American supernatural drama television series developed by Kevin Williamson and Julie Plec, based on the book series of the same name written by L. J. Smith. The series premiered on The CW on September 10, 2009, and concluded on March 10, 2017, having aired 171 episodes over eight seasons.

"I'm a vampire. I'm not supposed to feel anything. But I feel everything when I'm with you."

The show follows the life of Elena Gilbert (Nina Dobrev), a teenage girl who falls in love with a 162-year-old vampire named Stefan Salvatore (Paul Wesley). Their relationship becomes increasingly complicated as Stefan's mysterious older brother Damon Salvatore (Ian Somerhalder) returns, with a plan to bring back their past love Katherine Pierce, a vampire who looks exactly like Elena.

Seasons Overview

Season 1 (2009-2010)

Elena meets Stefan and discovers the supernatural world of Mystic Falls. The truth about vampires, werewolves, and witches is revealed.

Season 2 (2010-2011)

Katherine returns, and the Originals are introduced. The love triangle intensifies as Elena must choose between the Salvatore brothers.

Season 3 (2011-2012)

Stefan's dark side emerges as he's forced to work with Klaus. Elena's feelings for Damon grow stronger.

Season 4 (2012-2013)

Elena becomes a vampire and must adjust to her new life. The cure for vampirism is discovered.

Season 5 (2013-2014)

The travelers arrive in Mystic Falls. Damon and Elena's relationship faces new challenges.

Season 6 (2014-2015)

Elena is put into a magical sleep. The gang deals with the Gemini Coven and Kai Parker.

Season 7 (2015-2016)

Without Elena, the group faces new threats including the Heretics and Rayna Cruz, a vampire hunter.

Season 8 (2016-2017)

The final season brings the return of Katherine and a final battle that will determine the fate of Mystic Falls.

Originals

The Originals

The Originals are the first vampires ever created. They are the Mikaelson family: Klaus, Elijah, Rebekah, Kol, Finn, and their parents Mikael and Esther. These ancient vampires are the most powerful of their kind and play a significant role in The Vampire Diaries storyline.

Klaus Mikaelson

The hybrid Original vampire-werewolf, known for his power and ruthlessness. He is one of the most feared and powerful beings in the supernatural world.

Elijah Mikaelson

The noble Original vampire, known for his honor and loyalty to his family. He often serves as the voice of reason among the Originals.

Rebekah Mikaelson

The only female Original vampire, known for her strength and complex relationships. She seeks love and normalcy despite her immortality.

Legacies

Legacies

Legacies is a spin-off series that follows the next generation of supernatural beings at the Salvatore Boarding School for the Young & Gifted. The show focuses on Hope Mikaelson, the daughter of Klaus Mikaelson and Hayley Marshall, as well as other young supernatural beings learning to control their powers.

Hope Mikaelson

The tribrid daughter of Klaus Mikaelson and Hayley Marshall. She is part vampire, part werewolf, and part witch, making her one of the most powerful beings in existence.

Landon Kirby

Hope's love interest who discovers he is a phoenix, a rare supernatural being with the ability to resurrect from death.

Salvatore Boarding School

A school founded by Alaric Saltzman and Caroline Forbes to help young supernatural beings learn to control their powers and live peacefully among humans.

Fun Trivia

Did You Know?

  • The show was filmed in Covington, Georgia, which served as the fictional town of Mystic Falls.
  • Nina Dobrev played both Elena Gilbert and Katherine Pierce throughout the series.
  • The series has two spin-offs: The Originals and Legacies.
  • Ian Somerhalder (Damon) and Nina Dobrev (Elena) dated in real life during the show's run.
  • The show's theme song is "Never Say Never" by The Fray.
  • Paul Wesley (Stefan) directed several episodes of the series.
