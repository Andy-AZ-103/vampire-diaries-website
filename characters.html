Main Characters

Elena Gilbert

A human-turned-vampire who is the center of the Salvatore brothers' affections. Strong-willed and compassionate.

Stefan Salvatore

The "good" Salvatore brother who drinks only animal blood. He's kind, caring, and deeply in love with Elena.

Damon Salvatore

The "bad" Salvatore brother with a dark sense of humor. Despite his tough exterior, he has a good heart.

Caroline Forbes

Elena's best friend who becomes a vampire. She's organized, perfectionist, and fiercely loyal.

Bonnie Bennett

A powerful witch and Elena's best friend. She uses her magic to protect her loved ones.

Jeremy Gilbert

Elena's younger brother who becomes a vampire hunter. He's protective of his family and friends.
