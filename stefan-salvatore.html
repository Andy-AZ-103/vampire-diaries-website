Stefan Salvatore

Character Overview

Stefan Salvatore is the "good" Salvatore brother, known for his moral compass and his choice to drink only animal blood. He is kind, caring, and deeply in love with Elena Gilbert. Despite his efforts to be good, Stefan has a dark past as "The Ripper" that he constantly struggles with.

Background

Stefan was born on November 1, 1846, in Mystic Falls. He was turned into a vampire in 1864 by Katherine Pierce, who compelled him to forget. He spent decades trying to control his bloodlust and maintain his humanity, earning him the nickname "The Ripper" during his darker periods.

Personality

Stefan is known for his selfless nature and strong moral code. He chooses to drink animal blood instead of human blood, which makes him weaker but allows him to maintain his humanity. He often puts others' needs before his own and serves as a protector to those he cares about.

Relationships

  • Elena Gilbert - His first love and initial romantic interest
  • Damon Salvatore - His older brother, with whom he has a complicated relationship
  • Caroline Forbes - His eventual wife and true love
  • Katherine Pierce - The vampire who turned him and his first love

Portrayed By

Paul Wesley - Paul also directed several episodes of the series.
