Caroline Forbes

Character Overview

Caroline Forbes is Elena's best friend who becomes a vampire in Season 2. She is organized, perfectionist, and fiercely loyal. Initially portrayed as insecure and controlling, Caroline grows into a confident, strong vampire who learns to embrace her new nature and becomes a powerful ally.

Background

Caroline was born and raised in Mystic Falls. She was turned into a vampire by Katherine Pierce in Season 2. Initially struggling with her transformation, Caroline eventually learns to control her bloodlust and becomes one of the most stable and powerful vampires in the group. As a vampire, Caroline discovers new strengths including enhanced speed, strength, and the ability to compel humans. She becomes a master at controlling her vampire nature, often serving as a mentor to other new vampires. Her organizational skills and perfectionist nature make her an invaluable asset to the group, and she takes on leadership roles in protecting Mystic Falls.

Personality

Caroline is known for her organizational skills, attention to detail, and perfectionist nature. She is fiercely protective of her friends and family. As a vampire, she becomes more confident and assertive, using her strengths to help protect Mystic Falls. Her transformation from an insecure teenager to a powerful, confident vampire is one of the most significant character developments in the series.

Caroline's greatest strength is her ability to adapt and grow. She transforms her perfectionist tendencies from a source of insecurity into a powerful tool for organization and leadership. She becomes a natural leader, often taking charge in dangerous situations and making difficult decisions for the greater good. Her friendship with Elena and Bonnie is one of the strongest bonds in the series, and she consistently supports and protects them.

Throughout the series, Caroline navigates complex romantic relationships while maintaining her independence and strength. She learns to balance her vampire nature with her humanity, becoming one of the most well-adjusted vampires in the group. Caroline's journey from a controlling, insecure girl to a confident, powerful woman who finds true love with Stefan is a testament to her resilience and growth.

Relationships

  • Elena Gilbert - Her best friend
  • Stefan Salvatore - Her eventual husband and true love
  • Tyler Lockwood - Her first serious vampire relationship
  • Klaus Mikaelson - A complicated romantic relationship
  • Bonnie Bennett - Her close friend

Where She Is Now

Since The Vampire Diaries ended in 2017, Candice King has continued her successful acting career. She has appeared in various film and television projects, including reprising her role as Caroline Forbes in the spin-off series "Legacies."

Candice has also pursued her music career, releasing music and performing. She remains active in the entertainment industry and continues to take on diverse roles that showcase her talents as both an actress and performer. Her portrayal of Caroline Forbes remains one of the most beloved character arcs on the show.

Candice has also been involved in various philanthropic efforts and continues to maintain a strong connection with her fans. Her portrayal of Caroline Forbes, a character who transformed from insecurity to strength, remains one of the most iconic and beloved characters in supernatural television.
